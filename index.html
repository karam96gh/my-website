People & teamwork

Explain simply.
Lead with clarity.

Good software depends on how people learn and work together. These are the skills I bring to the classroom and to the team.

01
Teaching

Make ideas easier to understand

  • Break things down

    Start with an everyday example, then connect it to the code, one step at a time.

  • Meet people where they are

    Give beginners a clear starting point and experienced learners a useful challenge.

  • Learn by doing

    Turn each idea into an exercise or a small project, with room to experiment and ask questions.

  • Give useful feedback

    Review the attempt, explain the mistake and help the learner find a way forward.

02
Leadership

Help the team move with a plan

  • Make the goal clear

    Turn a client’s needs into understandable tasks, with a shared definition of done.

  • Set priorities

    Start with what matters most and divide delivery into manageable stages.

  • Build clear communication

    Make sure everyone knows their role, what they need from others and when to ask for help.

  • Work through blockers

    Discuss obstacles together and review the quality of the solution before moving on.
